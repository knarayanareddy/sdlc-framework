# Design brief

**For:** a 4–12 person team that already keeps a reading list in a group chat.
**Emotional register:** quick, unglamorous, trustworthy. Adjectives: clipped, legible, settled.
Anti-adjectives: playful, glossy, "AI-native".
**Material metaphor:** a shared paper list on a kitchen wall — one column, ruled lines,
crossed-off items. Not a card grid.
**What the audience already uses:** chat apps (dense, chronological), spreadsheets (row-per-item).
**Signature element:** each row is a ruled line with a small square checkbox that stays in
place while the title strikes through on mark-read. It serves the task (read state is the
thing people came for) and costs no layout shift.
**Banned defaults avoided:** no centred hero, no three icon cards, no purple gradient, no
rounded-2xl sea of cards, no emoji iconography.
